About

Hi, I'm Calvin Faris.

I'm an equity research analyst at Baird in St. Louis, covering U.S. banks for institutional investors. At heart I'm an investor — financially fluent, curious about how things work, and always looking for the signal in the noise.

I'm AI-forward in how I work and how I think. I build AI-assisted workflows that multiply what a small team can do, and I'm fascinated by where technology meets markets. What ties it together: I like connecting dots — between data and decisions, and between people and ideas.

I also build small things for the internet, like the St. Louis happy hour finder and golf course guide you'll find on this site.
