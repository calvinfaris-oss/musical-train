Coverage & listing quality

The expanded directory includes 70 entries, including additional West County and Sunset Hills venues around Des Peres. Forty-seven have an official page supporting a happy-hour program (some branch applicability, schedules or prices remain incomplete); twenty-three are guide-only leads hidden by default. Manual Des Peres searches use an approximate local center; visitors choose the radius themselves. Distances are straight-line, not driving distances. Coordinates are approximate; near-boundary results need map confirmation. This is not a census of every venue. Research checkpoints are October 1 and October 2, 2026; each card shows its own source-check date. Venues have not confirmed these offers directly. Schedules and prices are not refreshed automatically. Conflicting or incomplete windows are excluded from “Happening now,” as are all guide-only leads. Open-ended “until close” windows are shown but not treated as verified live matches. Multiple daytime windows are supported; late-night overnight matching is not inferred. Holiday closures and special events are not modeled. “Happening now” means the published schedule matches the current time, not a live open/closed guarantee. All listed venues use America/Chicago time. A source-check date means the source was accessed, not that the offer was newly published or confirmed in person. Known closure notices were used to remove stale leads, but the directory is not a venue census.

Location & privacy

No account or analytics are included. Saved venues stay in this browser only. Familiar St. Louis areas are resolved locally; other manual searches are sent to OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim service to locate US places. Precise browser location is used in memory to calculate distances and is not sent to the search service. Opening a venue map sends that venue’s approximate coordinates to OpenStreetMap; directions and menu links open external sites. Hosting providers and external services may keep standard access logs. Search results use approximate straight-line distances, not driving distances. Clear saved places with the button below.

Suggestions & corrections

There is no online submission service in this pilot. To report a changed offer, send the site owner the venue name and an official menu link. The owner can edit the listing data and redeploy the site. Do not send sensitive personal information.
